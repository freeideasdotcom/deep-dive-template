# Helping out

This idea belongs to everyone, so every kind of help counts:

- **Questions and thoughts:** open an issue. "Has anyone tried X?" is a great contribution.
- **Research:** add links, papers, videos or similar projects to [`docs/sources.md`](docs/sources.md).
- **Building:** pick an unchecked step in the README, say you're on it in an issue, then share what you learn (failures too).
- **Writing and design:** clearer explanations, sketches, diagrams and photos make ideas spread.

House rules:

1. Be kind. Assume good intent. Credit people.
2. Keep it free. Everything you add is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0](LICENSE).
3. Keep it safe and legal. No instructions for hurting people.

Started working on this idea? Add this project's link under **"go deeper"** on the original idea at [freeideasdotcom.com](https://freeideasdotcom.com) so others can find you.
